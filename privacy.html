Privacy policy

Scrollege Math · last updated 18 November 2026

Scrollege Math is a maths learning app for students, many of them under 18. We built it to collect as little as possible. In short: you can use it without signing up, we don't ask for your name, and there are no adverts and no tracking. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional, also if you subscribe; it keeps your progress and subscription if you change phone.

Your learning progress

To choose your next lessons, the app keeps a learning profile: which questions you answered, right or wrong, how hard they were, when, and whether you used a hint, replayed a lesson or skipped; plus the course, exam board, tier and target grade you chose. It is saved on your phone.

So your progress isn't lost, a copy is also saved on our server (run by our provider, Supabase). It is stored under a random ID the app creates, with no name, email or other details, so it does not say who you are. Only the app on your phone can read or change it. We use it only to run the app's learning features: never for advertising, never sold, never shared.

Signing in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google (Settings > Account), we receive from them an ID for your account and, if you share it, your email address. Apple lets you hide your real email. We never receive your password. Your progress is then saved under your account instead of the random ID, so it follows you to a new phone. We use your email only to show which account you're signed in with and to link your subscription to you: never for marketing, never sold, never shared.

Subscription

After a few free questions, Scrollege Math needs a subscription (a free month, then a monthly payment). You buy it through Apple's or Google's own in-app purchase, so we never see your card or payment details. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives the random ID (or your account's ID), what you bought and when, and basic technical details its service needs, such as your phone's operating system. We use this only to unlock the app for subscribers: never for advertising, never sold. Cancel or manage the subscription in your phone's settings; deleting your data in the app does not cancel it.

Saved clips

Clips you save (double-tap a video) are listed on your phone only. They are not sent anywhere, and "Delete my data" clears them.

Deleting your data

"Delete my data" at the bottom of the My progress screen, or "Delete account" in Settings when signed in, deletes your progress from your phone and from our server, along with the random ID or your account and its email address. Deleting the app removes the copy on your phone.

Videos

The videos stream from our storage provider, Supabase. Like any website, their servers see your device's IP address and basic request details when a video downloads. We don't use this to identify you.

"Save a copy" on the progress screen opens your phone's own share menu with a short text summary. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up needed, and no names or passwords. An email address only if you sign in with Apple or Google and share it.
  • No advertising, and no behavioural advertising.
  • No analytics and no tracking. The only outside service in the app, apart from our server, is RevenueCat for the subscription (above).
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • No social features: no public profiles, messages or comments.

Children

The app is designed for secondary school and sixth-form students and follows the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code): high privacy by default, and only the data the learning features need.

Changes

If we add anything that changes this, we will update this page and ask before collecting anything new.
