Scrollege Math support

Short maths videos, a quick question after each one, and a feed that brings back what you got wrong. GCSE Maths, A level Maths and A level Further Maths.

Contact us

Email support.scrollegemath@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Changing your course or goals

Open Settings (the sliders button at the top right of the feed), or tap the course name at the top left of the feed.

Stuck on a question?

Tap Stuck? under the answers, then Hint, Replay or Solution, or Continue to skip it for now: it comes back the next day.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone, with a copy on our server so it isn't lost. To keep it when you change or reset your phone, sign in with Apple or Google in Settings > Account, then sign in the same way on the new phone.

Subscription

Your first few questions are free. To keep going, start a free month; after that it's £3.99 a month until you cancel. It's paid through your Apple or Google account, not to us directly.

  • Cancel: on iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Scrollege Math. On Android, open the Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free month or the current month ends, and you won't be charged again. Deleting the app or your data doesn't cancel it.
  • New phone, or the app says you're not subscribed? Tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you paid with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play, not by us.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account (when signed in), or My progress > Delete my data. This deletes your progress, and your account if you have one, from your phone and our server straight away. If you can't open the app, email us and we'll help you delete it.

Found a mistake in a question or video?

Please tell us which question and what's wrong: support.scrollegemath@gmail.com.
