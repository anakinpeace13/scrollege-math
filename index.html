Scrollege Math Get the app

GCSE and A level maths, one short video at a time

Maths in 60-second videos. Swipe through short lessons, answer a quick question after each one, and the app brings back what you got wrong.

What's in it

  • GCSE Maths (Higher and Foundation), for AQA and Edexcel.
  • A level Maths: pure, statistics and mechanics.
  • A level Further Maths.
  • Original animated lessons, each followed by a question with a full worked explanation, and fresh practice questions when a topic comes back.

Built around how you learn

  • Every wrong answer explains the exact mistake behind it.
  • Topics you find hard come back as review questions a few days later.
  • Progress towards your target grade, topic by topic.
Private by design. No sign-up needed, no adverts, no tracking. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want your progress on a new phone.
